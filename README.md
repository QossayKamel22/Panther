## 🐆 Overview

**PANTHER** is a next-generation personal AI agent built to become more than a traditional chatbot.

Instead of simply answering questions, PANTHER understands your context, connects your digital ecosystem, identifies what matters, and helps you take action.

Think of it as your **second mind** — always available, context-aware, proactive, and built around you.

> **Think Ahead.**

---

## ✨ What Makes PANTHER Different?

Traditional assistants wait for commands.

PANTHER is designed to understand **context**.

It can connect information from your:

- 📅 Calendar
- 📧 Email
- ✅ Tasks
- 📝 Notes
- 📁 Documents
- 🌐 Web
- 💬 Conversations
- 🧠 Personal Memory

Then turn that context into useful actions and insights.

### Example

Instead of asking:

> "What meetings do I have today?"

You can ask:

> "What should I know before my meeting?"

PANTHER can understand the meeting, review relevant context, identify important information, and prepare you for it.

# 🚀 Core Features

### 🧠 Context Awareness

PANTHER understands conversations and connected information instead of treating every request as an isolated question.

### 🗂️ Personal Memory

Remember important preferences, projects, decisions, and context to create a more personalized experience.

### ⚡ Proactive Intelligence

Identify important events, deadlines, changes, and information that may require your attention.

### 🔎 Intelligent Search

Search across connected sources and the web to find, understand, and summarize information.

### 🤖 AI Agent

PANTHER can use tools and execute tasks instead of simply generating text.

### 🔗 Connected Ecosystem

Designed to connect your most important digital services into one intelligent layer.

### 🔐 User Control

Actions that affect your accounts or data should require explicit authorization.

# 💬 Example Scenarios

### Morning Briefing

> **You:**  
> "Panther, what's important today?"

PANTHER analyzes your schedule, tasks, messages, and priorities.

---

### Meeting Preparation

> **You:**  
> "Prepare me for my 2 PM meeting."

PANTHER gathers relevant context and creates a concise briefing.

---

### Decision Making

> **You:**  
> "Think this through with me."

PANTHER analyzes the situation, compares options, identifies risks, and helps you make a decision.

---

### Personal Memory

> **You:**  
> "Remember that this project is my top priority."

PANTHER stores the relevant context for future interactions.

---

### Taking Action

> **You:**  
> "Handle this email."

PANTHER understands the request, prepares the action, and asks for confirmation when required.
